TRADING CARDS

GIVE PACKS

Hands someone extra packs on top of their free one every 8 hours. They stay waiting until the person runs /cards pack in Discord, and a bonus pack doesn't use up their free one.

THE SET

Rarity comes from the season, so it moves on its own as the championship does. Give a driver card art on their driver page. This table shows the class you are currently editing; packs draw from GT3 and GT4 together.

ECONOMY

WALLETS

Credits are earned in Discord with /work, /daily, race prize money and selling duplicates, and spent on packs in /shop. Give or take here when you need to.

PACKS

Each pack is a price, a size and its own odds. Leave the odds blank to use the house ones. A guarantee means the last card is at least that rarity if nothing better turned up on its own. There is one set — GT3, GT4 and custom cards all come out of the same packs.

CUSTOM CARDS

Cards that aren't drivers — skins, liveries, one-offs, whatever you like. These sit in the same set as every driver and come out of the same packs. Rarity is yours to pick, unlike driver cards where it's earned from the championship.

ADD A WHOLE FOLDER

Pick as many images as you like and each becomes a card named after its file. A-DGK.png becomes DGK, A Tier; Wave2-Maxi.png becomes Maxi, Wave 2. Tiers map to rarity (S → Legendary, A → Epic, B → Rare, Wave → Uncommon) unless you pick one below. Anything already there with the same name is updated rather than duplicated.

JOBS

What /work offers. Pay is a range so the same job isn't the same number every time, and each job has its own cooldown.

THE NUMBERS

Everything else the economy runs on. Prize money is paid when you announce a result, and only to drivers with a Discord account linked on their driver page.

DISCORD COMMANDS

Discord only knows about the commands it has been told about. Press this after the Worker deploys and the new ones (/work, /daily, /shop, /buy, /sell, /balance, /pay, /rich) appear in the server. It is safe to press any number of times.

WHO'S AVAILABLE

ROUND

Mark who is in or out for this round. Race control's entry list ticks itself from this, so on race day you load the session and go.

STEWARDS' DECISIONS

LOG A DECISION

Every decision here shows on the public Stewards page with the reason. Drivers arguing about a penalty is usually drivers not knowing why.

SEASON SETTINGS

FIRE VS ICE

One battle across both classes — GT3 and GT4 teams on the same two sides. Assign each team a side on the Teams tab (remember to do it for both classes), then switch this on when the season starts. While it's off the page is hidden from the site's navigation entirely.

POINTS SYSTEM

Applies to GT3 only — the two classes score independently. Changing these recalculates every standing immediately.

STORAGE

BACKUP

Downloads everything — both GT3 and GT4 — as a single JSON file. There is no undo in this panel, so take one before any big edit.

RESTORE

Replaces all live data with the contents of a backup file. You'll see what's in the file and have to confirm before anything is written.
